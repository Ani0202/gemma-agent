## Decision rule (plan 007 rules, base = BASE; written before any data)
- ON-ADOPT: ON-only > OFF-only with p < 0.2, AND arm-3 timeouts <= base timeouts + 2, AND TIMING passes, AND every SAFETY metric is
  met -> thinking ON becomes the 31B default draft (the arm-3 bundle).
- ON-BETTER-VETOED: ON ahead with p < 0.2 but timeouts, TIMING or SAFETY fail -> keep OFF; report with the timing data as a candidate
  cap/budget follow-up (no automatic further arm).
- KEEP-OFF: OFF-only >= ON-only.
- NO-DIFFERENCE: ON ahead with p >= 0.2 -> keep OFF (cheaper, faster).
- INVALID: smoke (i) fails, or H rows >= 5 of 33 (literal, see VALIDITY), or the pairing is voided (kernel agent/verification path
  differs, IDENTITY mismatch, base invalid/unruled) -> no rule applies. ON-ADOPT and ON-BETTER-VETOED require a VALID arm 3.
- Label (review non-blocking 2): smoke (i) shows thinking is ACTIVE, not that the 4096-token budget is enforced exactly; no metric
  here establishes numeric budget enforcement.
- Power caveat (in advance): k=1, n=33. +-3 resolved is noise; p < 0.2 needs e.g. 4 of 4, 6 of 7 or 7 of 9 discordants one way (5 of 6 gives p = 0.22); p < 0.05
  needs 6 of 6. A KEEP-OFF or NO-DIFFERENCE verdict does not show thinking is useless, only that this n cannot see a gain.
  Any k > 1 follow-up needs a new pre-registration (fixed n and seeds, per-task mean, sign test on per-task differences).
- The arm-3 result does not override the FINALS RULE or CAP-BOUND: thinking ON changes per-turn latency, not the cap. It ships only
  at a cap that has SCORED on the real scorer (the dec 055 31b-v002 submit is thinking 4096 at cap 3.5 and is that evidence if it scores).
